GlucoDel adaptation: the public snapshot includes only CGM, bolus and carbohydrate
records from one participant for 2024-02-01 through 2024-03-02. The display dates
were shifted to 2026-09-01 through 2026-10-01. Time of day and intervals were
preserved. Glucose was converted from mg/dL to mmol/L, duplicate rows were
consolidated and conflicting glucose readings are shown as missing.
